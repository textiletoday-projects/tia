The Award

Innovation deserves recognition

Recognition creates an appetite to innovate, and that appetite becomes an organisation's inner strength.

The textile and apparel industry is moving fast, pushed by new technology, shifting buyer demand and the global need for sustainable practice. Staying competitive means innovating everywhere: in product development, process optimisation and sustainable manufacturing.

Launched in 2024, the Textile Innovation Award celebrates the organisations setting new standards in Bangladesh's textile sector. The second edition is held on the second night of the TIE-ITET Textile Innovation Expo 2026, at the same venue.

Independent recognition

Every nomination is reviewed by a Jury Board drawn from the industry's academic, manufacturing and institutional leadership.

Credibility by association

Presented by Textile Innovation Exchange with ITET, in association with BKMEA and BGBA, implemented by Textile Today Innovation Hub.

A stage, not a certificate

Winners are recognised on Awards Night at ICCB, Dhaka, in front of around 350 senior industry guests.

Innovation made visible

Judged on five published criteria, your nomination turns the work into documented evidence buyers and peers can see.

Recognition categories

Eight ways to be recognised

Nominations are open across the value chain, from the factory floor to people and planet. Each card lists what the Jury Board looks for. Categories are awarded on merit, so not every category is awarded each year.

How nominations are judged

Five criteria. Evidence for each.

Every nomination is assessed by the independent Jury Board against the same five criteria. Build your submission so each one is answered with evidence.

Impact

The measurable difference made: results, savings, revenue, quality, people or environmental outcomes.

Scalability

Whether the approach can be repeated across lines, plants or the wider industry with available resources.

Creativity

What is new or different compared with common practice, and the unmet need it answers.

Sustainability

The environmental and social benefit, and whether the gains will last.

Market Relevance

How well the innovation answers real buyer, customer or industry needs, now and next.

A note for nominees. Nominate the work, not the company. Choose the category where your evidence is strongest, and support every claim with before-and-after data, photos or documents.

Eligibility, rules and disclaimer

Nominations are made for an organisation or company in textile, apparel, allied industries or services, with an innovation that fits one of the specified categories.

The Textile Innovation Award is organised to recognise excellence and innovation within the textile and apparel industry. Participation is voluntary, and all nominations are subject to review by the Jury Board. The decisions of the jury are final and based on the information provided in the nomination. The organisers and the Jury Board reserve the right to modify categories, criteria and rules at any time without prior notice. Depending on the merits of nominations, not all categories are awarded each year.

The organisers do not certify the accuracy, completeness or suitability of information submitted by nominees, and are not liable for inaccuracies or misrepresentations. Participants are responsible for the authenticity of the information they provide.

By participating, nominees agree that the organisers may use their names, logos and submitted materials to promote the awards without further consent or compensation. The organisers may disqualify any participant found in violation of the award's terms or engaging in unethical practice.

The story so far · First edition

The 2024 honour roll

The first edition was presented on 26 October 2024 at Dhaka Regency Hotel. The Advisor to the Ministry of Jute and Textiles attended as Chief Guest, with the Presidents of BKMEA and BTMA as special guests.

Four organisations took the stage in 2024 for work in product development, market development, garment processing and people development. In 2026 the Award grows to eight categories and moves to ICCB, alongside Bangladesh's largest gathering of textile innovators.
